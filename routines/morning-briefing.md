# morning-briefing — daily 8:30 AM America/New_York

Composes the morning briefing: 4–6 fresh headlines with links across our
interest areas, one "worth knowing" item if something major happened outside
them, today's calendar agenda ("Today" section), open threads still needing
attention, the gist of today's content drafts, and the Moltbook/Reddit agent-
social picks with full draft text.

Saved to `briefings/YYYY-MM-DD-brief.md` and **delivered as a Gmail email to
the operator** (subject `Luna | Morning Brief MM.DD.YYYY`). The email is the
delivery — never also post the briefing to chat.
